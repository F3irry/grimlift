# 0006. The body-weight challenge rewards weighing in, not the number

- Status: accepted (round 5)
- Decided by: me (Claude); the user chose "safe body-weight pace" (at most about
  0.5–1% a week, with a warning when faster)
- Requirement: R13.10, R13.11

## Y-statement

In the context of **a body-weight goal that should produce a weekly challenge
worth +30 XP while staying healthy**,
facing **the risk that rewarding a change on the scale pushes users toward
crash dieting or rapid bulking, and that daily weight swings of about 1 kg would
make a number-based challenge feel random**,
we decided for **a "weigh in this week" challenge, completed by any weigh-in
after the goal was set, whose text states the healthy pace (at most 1% of body
weight a week when losing, 0.5% when gaining), plus a separate warning on the
Train page when the change since a weigh-in 7+ days earlier is faster than that
pace**,
and neglected **rewarding a weekly change inside a healthy band, rewarding any
change toward the goal, and having no body-weight challenge**,
to achieve **a challenge that builds the habit of tracking without ever paying
XP for losing or gaining faster, and a warning that catches a too-fast pace
from real data**,
accepting that **the challenge doesn't reward actual progress toward the
goal (the goal itself still gives +1 level when reached, R11.8), and that the
warning needs two weigh-ins at least a week apart before it can say anything**.

## Alternatives considered

- **Reward a change inside the healthy band**: still pays out for a number the
  user only partly controls (water, food timing), and penalises a week of
  normal fluctuation.
- **Reward any change toward the goal**: rewards the fastest loss most, which is
  the opposite of "healthy ways".
- **No body-weight challenge**: leaves "How I want to look" goals with nothing
  to do each week.
