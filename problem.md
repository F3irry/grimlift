# Grimlift

## Problem
When I don't have a clear head or I feel embarrassed at the gym, I get shy and lose
motivation. Tracking workouts and meals feels out of the way, and it's hard to figure
out what to do for each body part to reach my goals. Progress feels invisible, so it's
easy to stop showing up.

## Initial requirements
- R1: It is easy to log a workout: exercise, weight, and reps.
- R2: Completing a workout earns XP, so progress feels like leveling up in a video game.
- R3: The user levels up when they hit a new personal best on an exercise.
- R4: Logged workouts, XP, and level are still there after closing and reopening the app.
